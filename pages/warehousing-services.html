Strategic Warehousing and Asset Management

Modern supply chains demand more than just storage space; they require intelligent management. Linking Bridges operates state-of-the-art logistics hubs equipped to handle everything from raw industrial materials to fragile electronics and cold-chain perishables.

Our facilities are strategically located near major ports and transport corridors across the Kingdom, minimizing transit times and lowering drayage costs. We act as your central distribution node, ensuring products are stored safely and dispatched accurately.

Facility Capabilities

  • Cloud-based WMS: Live inventory tracking, barcode scanning, and automated stock alerts.
  • Climate-Controlled Zones: Scalable ambient, chilled, and frozen storage environments.
  • Cross-Docking Services: Rapid transfer of goods from inbound to outbound transport with minimal storage time.
  • High-Security Infrastructure: 24/7 CCTV, access control systems, and round-the-clock physical security patrols.
  • Value-Added Services: In-house kitting, labeling, repacking, and palletization prior to dispatch.
